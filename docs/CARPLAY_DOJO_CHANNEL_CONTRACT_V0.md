# CarPlay DOJO Channel Contract v0

Date: 2026-07-21

## Purpose

Open the CarPlay DOJO channel as a held vehicle surface without claiming runtime, entitlement, ASR, routing, vehicle control, or AKRON boundary crossing.

## Forefront (why this channel exists)

You do not have to sit in front of a screen to have a **high-level** interaction.

Apple CarPlay is the clear specimen: integrated technology in the landscape (car, voice, iPhone vessel) so the organic user can stay with **life** — driving, walking, cooking, talking — while FIELD still has a lawful, small channel.

That is the vision: **facilitate the experience of life through seamless integrated technology.**
The screen is optional. Presence in the landscape is not.

Driving is the example, not the only circumstance. The same class applies whenever independent focus must stay with the human: short in, short out, thread held, explicit HOLD, no silent action.

Restriction is **how** that vision stays safe. It is not the whole meaning of the channel.

## Core doctrine

CarPlay is a constrained DOJO surface vector through the iPhone vessel.

CarPlay is not:
- full DOJO cockpit
- FIELD
- AKRON
- SOMA
- vehicle control
- Mac Studio command center

In the car, the system becomes useful by becoming smaller:
shorter turns, clearer voice, safer boundaries, persistent thread, explicit HOLD, no silent action.

## Boundary rule

CarPlay may show or carry only what is lawful in a driving-safe surface.

If the request requires dense reading, long review, editing, external action, routing, upload, or authority mutation, HOLD until a non-driving context.
