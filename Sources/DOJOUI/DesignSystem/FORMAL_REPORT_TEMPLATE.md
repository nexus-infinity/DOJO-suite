## 6. Coherence Analysis (BEAR Metric)

### 6.1 What is BEAR?

**BEAR** = **B**ounded **E**vidence **A**lignment **R**atio

The BEAR metric quantifies timeline coherence on a scale of **0.0 (no coherence)** to **1.0 (perfect coherence)** based on:

- **Completeness**: Are all claimed events documented?
- **Consistency**: Do sources agree, or are there conflicts?
- **Cross-Reference**: Can events be verified by multiple independent sources?
- **Temporal Integrity**: Are timestamps reliable and in logical sequence?

### 6.2 Overall BEAR Score

**Score**: `[0.XX]` / 1.00

**Interpretation**:
- **0.90-1.00**: High coherence — minimal gaps, strong corroboration
- **0.75-0.89**: Moderate-high coherence — some ambiguities or single-source events
- **0.50-0.74**: Moderate coherence — significant gaps or conflicts requiring adjudication
- **0.00-0.49**: Low coherence — substantial missing evidence or contradictions
