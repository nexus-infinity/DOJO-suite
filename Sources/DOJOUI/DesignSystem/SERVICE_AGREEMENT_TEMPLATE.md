This agreement establishes the terms under which the Investigator will perform forensic investigation and evidence reconstruction services for the Client, following the **DOJO Suite methodology** — a structured, geometry-driven approach to temporal truth validation.

### 1.1 Service Phases (Tesla 3-6-9 Cycle)

All engagements proceed through **three mandatory phases**, with validation gates between each:

#### **Phase 3: Evidence Audit (Intake/Observe)**
- **Duration**: [X] business days from contract execution
- **Activities**:
  - Structured intake of all client-provided documentation (see Appendix A: Document Intake Checklist)
  - Cryptographic anchoring of all evidence (hash generation, timestamp verification)
  - Initial timeline mapping (event identification, temporal anchor extraction)
  - Scope validation (confirm engagement boundaries, identify gaps)
- **Deliverable**: Evidence Audit Report (preliminary findings, timeline scaffold, identified gaps)
- **Validation Gate**: Client approval required to proceed to Phase 6

#### **Phase 6: Reconstruction (Process)**
- **Duration**: [X] business days from Phase 3 approval
- **Activities**:
  - Temporal reconstruction (event sequencing, conflict resolution, gap analysis)
  - Cross-reference validation (document correlation, witness statement verification)
  - Metadata forensics (digital evidence analysis, authenticity verification)
  - Coherence scoring (BEAR metric calculation for timeline confidence)
- **Deliverable**: Reconstruction Report (validated timeline, evidence matrix, coherence metrics)
- **Validation Gate**: Client review and approval to proceed to Phase 9

#### **Phase 9: Synthesis (Deliver)**
- **Duration**: [X] business days from Phase 6 approval
- **Activities**:
  - Formal report generation (executive summary, findings, supporting evidence)
  - Regulatory filing preparation (if applicable)
  - Client presentation and Q&A
  - Handoff of all work product (reports, evidence database, cryptographic signatures)
- **Deliverable**: Final Investigation Report (see Appendix B: Report Template Structure)
- **Cycle Completion**: Client signs off on deliverables

## Appendix C: DOJO Suite Methodology Overview

### What is the DOJO Suite?

The **DOJO Suite** is a geometry-driven forensic investigation framework based on **Tesla 3-6-9 phase logic**:

- **Phase 3 (Intake/Observe)**: Evidence gathering and initial validation
- **Phase 6 (Process)**: Temporal reconstruction and coherence analysis
- **Phase 9 (Synthesize)**: Formal reporting and delivery

### Key Principles

1. **Non-Bypassability**: All phases must be completed sequentially (no shortcuts)
2. **Cryptographic Anchoring**: All evidence cryptographically hashed and timestamped
3. **Sovereignty**: Client retains full ownership and control of data
4. **Coherence Scoring**: Timeline confidence measured via BEAR metric (0.0-1.0)
5. **PDCA Integration**: Continuous quality improvement through Plan-Do-Check-Act cycles

### BEAR Coherence Metric

**BEAR** = **B**ounded **E**vidence **A**lignment **R**atio

- **1.0**: Perfect coherence (all evidence agrees, no conflicts)
- **0.75-0.99**: High coherence (minor gaps or ambiguities)
- **0.50-0.74**: Moderate coherence (some conflicts requiring adjudication)
- **0.00-0.49**: Low coherence (significant gaps, contradictions, or missing evidence)

Reports include BEAR scores for overall timeline and individual event clusters.
